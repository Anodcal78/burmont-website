Detailed Empty-Home Cleaning

What Move Cleaning Can Include

Empty spaces allow access to areas that are difficult to reach during regular cleaning. We confirm the final checklist and property condition before quoting.

Kitchens

Cabinet interiors and exteriors when empty, countertops, sinks, appliance exteriors, fixtures, baseboards, and floors.

Bathrooms

Showers, tubs, toilets, vanities, sinks, mirrors, fixtures, baseboards, and floors prepared for the next occupant.

Rooms & Floors

Reachable surfaces, shelving, doors and trim as agreed, closets, vacuuming, and mopping throughout the property.

Property note: Personal belongings, trash, and large debris should be removed before cleaning. The property should have working utilities and safe access.

For Residents

Move With One Less Task

Coordinate your cleaning after belongings are removed and before keys change hands whenever possible. Share any lease, sale, or move timing that affects access.

For Property Managers

Prepare for the Next Occupant

  • Property-specific priority checklist
  • Target completion date
  • Direct communication about visible concerns

Questions

Move Cleaning FAQs

Does the property need to be empty?

Empty or nearly empty is best. Tell us if belongings remain so we can confirm the scope and access.

Do you remove furniture or trash?

No. Furniture, personal belongings, and large trash or debris should be removed before cleaning.

Can property managers request recurring turnover service?

Yes. We are open to ongoing relationships with property managers, subject to location, scope, and scheduling.
