Detailed Attention

What a Deep Cleaning Can Cover

We tailor the checklist to your home and priorities. The final scope is confirmed during the estimate.

Kitchen Details

More focused cleaning of cabinet fronts, appliance exteriors, backsplashes, fixtures, baseboards, and built-up areas.

Bathrooms

Detailed attention to showers, tubs, fixtures, vanities, toilets, mirrors, baseboards, and commonly missed surfaces.

Throughout the Home

Deep vs. Recurring

The Right Starting Point

Recurring cleaning maintains an agreed standard on a regular schedule. Deep cleaning devotes more time to built-up areas and details that are not part of every maintenance visit.

If your goal is ongoing support, we can discuss beginning with a deep cleaning and moving into weekly, biweekly, or monthly residential service.

Before the Visit

Help Us Focus on Cleaning

  • Share your highest-priority rooms and concerns
  • Clear excess clutter from surfaces when possible
  • Tell us about pets, access, or product sensitivities
  • Confirm any requested add-on details in advance

Questions

Deep Cleaning FAQs

How is deep cleaning different from recurring cleaning?

Deep cleaning focuses on built-up areas and detailed tasks that require more time. Recurring service maintains the home on a regular schedule.

Do you require a deep clean before recurring service?

Not always. We’ll learn about the condition of the home and recommend the best starting point.

Can I request specific priority areas?

Yes. Tell us what matters most, and we’ll confirm what can be included in the quote and available time.
