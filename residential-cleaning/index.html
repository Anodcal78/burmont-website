A Dependable Routine

What Recurring Cleaning Can Cover

We’ll agree on the priorities for your home before service begins. A typical recurring visit focuses on the areas that make the biggest day-to-day difference.

Kitchens

Countertops, sinks, appliance exteriors, cabinet fronts as needed, and the floors that collect everyday traffic.

Bathrooms

Toilets, tubs, showers, sinks, mirrors, fixtures, and floors cleaned with careful attention to high-use areas.

Living Areas & Bedrooms

Dusting, reachable surfaces, vacuuming, mopping, and general straightening for a refreshed, comfortable home.

Questions

Recurring Cleaning FAQs

How often can you clean my home?

We are open to weekly, biweekly, and monthly recurring schedules. Availability depends on your location, preferred day, and the needs of the home.

Do I need a deep cleaning first?

Not every home does. We’ll learn about the current condition of the home and recommend the best starting point before service begins.

Can my cleaning plan change over time?

Yes. Tell us when your priorities or schedule change, and we’ll discuss how to adjust the plan.

What towns do you serve?

We serve communities throughout Delaware County and the Main Line, including Bryn Mawr, Wayne, Havertown, Newtown Square, Ardmore, and Media.
