HOW TO ADD YOUR REAL GOOGLE REVIEWS

You will edit index.html on the feature/seo-service-pages branch.

1. Open index.html.
2. Click the pencil icon to edit.
3. Press Ctrl+F and search for:
   What Our Clients Say

You will see three review blocks similar to this:

<figure class="review-card">
  <div class="stars" aria-label="Five star rating">★★★★★</div>
  <blockquote>“Dependable, thorough, and always on time.”</blockquote>
  <figcaption>— Katie M.<br><span>Wayne</span></figcaption>
</figure>

Replace each complete <figure>...</figure> block with a real review.

EXAMPLE FORMAT:

<figure class="review-card">
  <div class="stars" aria-label="Five star Google review">★★★★★</div>
  <blockquote>“PASTE THE CUSTOMER'S REAL REVIEW HERE.”</blockquote>
  <figcaption>— FIRST NAME + LAST INITIAL<br><span>Google Review</span></figcaption>
</figure>

Use:
- The customer’s real public review wording
- Their public Google display name, shortened to first name and last initial if desired
- “Google Review” underneath unless you genuinely know their town

Do not use made-up towns or rewrite the customer’s meaning.

ADD A GOOGLE REVIEWS BUTTON

Immediately after the closing </div> for the three review cards, add:

<div class="centered" style="margin-top:24px;">
  <a class="btn btn-primary" href="PASTE-YOUR-GOOGLE-REVIEWS-LINK-HERE"
     target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
    Read All Google Reviews <span class="cta-arrow" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
  </a>
</div>

HOW TO GET THE GOOGLE REVIEWS LINK

1. Open your Burmont Cleaning Co. Google Business Profile in Google Search.
2. Click “Read reviews.”
3. Copy the URL from the browser address bar.
4. Paste that URL in place of:
   PASTE-YOUR-GOOGLE-REVIEWS-LINK-HERE
