A Detailed Finish

What Post-Construction Cleaning Can Include

Each project is quoted according to its size, condition, and readiness. The exact scope is confirmed before work begins.

Dust & Surfaces

Detailed dust removal from reachable horizontal surfaces, trim, doors, fixtures, ledges, and other finished areas.

Cabinets & Rooms

Cleaning inside and outside empty cabinets, plus detailed attention to completed kitchens, bathrooms, and living or work areas.

Floors & Final Presentation

Vacuuming and mopping appropriate finished floors so the completed property is ready for occupancy or presentation.

Scope note: This service is final/detail cleaning after construction work is complete. It does not include heavy construction debris removal, hazardous materials, or contractor punch-list work.

Before We Arrive

The Space Should Be Ready for Cleaning

Construction work should be complete, contractors should be finished in the cleaning area, and large debris and tools should be removed. Utilities and safe access must be available.

Because construction dust can settle after it is disturbed, some projects may benefit from more than one phase or a follow-up visit. We’ll discuss that during the estimate if the condition of the property calls for it.

Questions

Post-Construction Cleaning FAQs

Is this construction debris removal?

No. Our service is final and detail cleaning. Contractors or property owners should remove large debris, building materials, tools, and hazardous items before we arrive.

When should I schedule the cleaning?

Contact us before your target occupancy date, but plan the cleaning for after all construction work and contractor touch-ups are complete.

Do you clean both homes and commercial spaces?

Yes. We consider homes, renovations, rental properties, and smaller commercial projects within our service area.

Can you clean inside cabinets?

Yes, empty cabinet interiors and exteriors can be included in the agreed project scope.
